Support

Email support@openweek.app and tell us what you were doing and what happened. Please don’t include health or money details.

Can’t get in?

There are no passwords. Use any sign-in on your account: Apple, Google, or a link to your email. If an email link says it has expired, ask for a new one and open it on the same phone.

Add or change how you sign in

In the app: Settings, Profile, Sign-in methods.

Get a copy of your data

In the app: Settings, Privacy and data, Export my data.

Delete your account

In the app: Settings, Privacy and data, Delete account. If you can’t get in, email us from an address on your account and we’ll help.
